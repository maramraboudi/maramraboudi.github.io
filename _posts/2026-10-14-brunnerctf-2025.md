I was proud to take part in **BrunnerCTF 2025** as a member of **EagleDefenders**, competing alongside teammates in an international cybersecurity challenge. The event brought teams together to solve challenges across a range of security topics, and gave us a great opportunity to learn, collaborate, and test our skills under pressure.

Our team solved **53 challenges** and earned **6,531 points**, placing **36th overall out of 1,148 teams**, **30th internationally out of 1,065 teams**, and **2nd among Tunisian teams**.

The challenges covered a broad mix of categories: **Boot2Root, forensics, OSINT, cryptography, reverse engineering, web, mobile, Pwn, Misc, and Shake & Bake**. Moving between these areas meant sharing ideas, drawing on each teammate's strengths, and staying curious when a challenge took an unexpected turn.

More than the rankings, I valued the teamwork and the chance to learn from an international CTF community. BrunnerCTF was a memorable experience that encouraged me to keep practicing, explore unfamiliar categories, and grow together with EagleDefenders.
