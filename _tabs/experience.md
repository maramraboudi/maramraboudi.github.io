## Cybersecurity Intern

**EY Tunisia** · June 1 – July 31, 2026

During this experience, I had the opportunity to work on **VulnTrack**, a vulnerability and compliance management platform designed to improve how security teams manage vulnerabilities, assets, risks, and compliance activities.

I contributed to the development of a secure platform addressing real-world security challenges, including **vulnerability management, secure data ingestion, risk analysis, compliance tracking, and application security**. I also worked on security mechanisms such as **brute-force protection, XML security, audit trails, and automated testing**, while contributing to the implementation of compliance features aligned with **ISO 27001 and SOC 2**.

This internship allowed me to strengthen my knowledge of **secure software development and application security**, while gaining a better understanding of how cybersecurity requirements are translated into real software solutions. I also developed my skills in **Python, Django, vulnerability management, risk assessment, security testing, compliance, and software engineering**.

Beyond the technical side, working on a real project taught me the importance of **teamwork, problem-solving, communication, code quality, and thinking about security from the beginning of the development lifecycle**.

## CTF Player, Author & Content Creator

**EagleDefenders** · August 2025 – Present

As a member of **EagleDefenders**, a cybersecurity CTF team, I contribute to learning activities and competitions as a player, challenge author, and content creator. I design and solve challenges across **digital forensics, OSINT, cryptography, and web security**, and create write-ups and educational content to share what I learn with the community.

Taking part in national and international CTF competitions has strengthened my **problem-solving and critical-thinking skills**. It has also given me practical exposure to vulnerability assessment, penetration-testing tools, and incident-response workflows.
