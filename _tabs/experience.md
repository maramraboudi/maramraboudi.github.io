## Cybersecurity Intern

**EY Tunisia** · June 1 – July 31, 2026

During my two-month internship, I contributed to **VulnTrack**, a vulnerability and compliance management project created to replace fragmented spreadsheet- and email-based tracking with a centralized, audit-ready process. The project helped teams organize security findings, follow remediation work, and communicate risk clearly to both technical and non-technical stakeholders.

This experience gave me valuable insight into cybersecurity consulting and the importance of clear communication, accountability, and compliance in client-focused work. It also helped me learn how to manage my responsibilities, collaborate with teammates, and contribute in a professional environment.
